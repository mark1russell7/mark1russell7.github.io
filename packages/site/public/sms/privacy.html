Mark Russell Alerts: privacy policy

This policy applies to the text messages of Mark Russell Alerts.

The data that the program keeps

For each recipient, the program keeps the phone number, the date of the verbal consent, the opt-out record, and the alerts that it sent. The recipients are Mark Russell and the friends and family who asked Mark Russell for the alerts.

How the program uses the data

The program uses a phone number only to send the alerts that the recipient asked for, and to obey HELP and STOP.

Sharing

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The program does not sell, rent or share phone numbers or consent data. The text messaging provider of the program processes the numbers only to deliver the messages.

How to stop and how to delete

Reply STOP to any message of the program to stop all messages. To ask for the deletion of your data, tell Mark Russell, or use the contact on the GitHub profile of Mark Russell.
