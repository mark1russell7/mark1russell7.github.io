Mark Russell Alerts: how to opt in

Mark Russell Alerts is a one-way text alert program. It sends alerts only to the phone of its owner, Mark Russell.

What the program sends

A self-hosted alert service sends short notices to the owner. The notices are severe weather warnings for the area of the owner, outages of the home services of the owner, and failures of the software jobs of the owner. The program sends no marketing and no promotions. It is not open to the public.

How the owner gives consent

The opt-in is a keyword by text message:

  1. The owner texts the keyword START to the toll-free number +1 (844) 844-2406.
  2. The number replies at once with a confirmation. The reply tells how to get help and how to stop, and that message and data rates can apply.
  3. After the confirmation, the alert service sends alerts to that phone number only.

Frequency, help and stop

  • Message frequency varies with events. Most days have 0 to 5 messages.
  • Message and data rates can apply.
  • Text HELP for help. Text STOP to stop all messages.
