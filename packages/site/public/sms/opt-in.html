Mark Russell Alerts: how a person opts in

Mark Russell Alerts is a private, one-way text alert program. Only Mark Russell and the friends and family who ask Mark Russell for the alerts get them. The public cannot sign up.

What the program sends

A self-hosted alert service sends short notices: severe weather warnings for the area of the recipient, outages of the home services of the owner, and failures of the software jobs of the owner. The program sends no marketing and no promotions.

How a person gives consent

The opt-in is verbal. The program has no sign-up form and no sign-up keyword.

  1. A person asks Mark Russell for the alerts, in person or in a direct conversation.
  2. Mark Russell reads the consent script below to the person, and the person says yes.
  3. Mark Russell adds the phone number of the person to the alert list, with the date of the consent.
  4. Only the numbers on that list get alerts.

The consent script

"Mark Russell Alerts sends you text alerts about severe weather and about outages. Message frequency varies, and most days have 0 to 5 messages. Message and data rates can apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop, and reply HELP for help. The terms and the privacy policy are at mark1russell7.github.io/sms. Do you want these alerts?"

Frequency, help and stop

  • Message frequency varies with events. Most days have 0 to 5 messages.
  • Message and data rates can apply.
  • Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to stop all messages.
